About response times

Response times are targets for when we start work on a reported problem, not for when it is fixed. They are counted in business hours, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern. We don't offer 24/7 coverage yet.

Fees you pay directly

You pay hosting, backend, app store and other third-party fees (such as email, text messages and card processing) directly to those providers, at cost, from accounts in your own business name. For example, the Apple Developer Program is currently $99 a year and Google Play registration is a one-time $25. These are set by Apple and Google and can change.

How we work

Careful by design.

  • AI-assisted, human-approved. We're an AI-assisted web studio. AI agents draft designs, code and text and run automated checks; a person reviews and approves every page before you see it, and nothing goes live without your written OK. AI-made text and images can contain errors, so please check all facts, such as prices, hours and claims, before you approve.
  • Built on a private test site. Nothing goes public until you sign off.
  • A person reviews every page for facts, images and image rights before you see it.
  • Accessibility. We build with accessibility best practices in mind and check each site against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated scans and a hands-on review. You get a short report of what we checked.
  • Security. HTTPS, spam protection on forms, two-step login on your accounts, and card payments handled by the payment platform, never stored by us.
  • If something goes wrong. If we discover a security incident that affects your data, we'll tell you promptly, and no later than 72 hours after we discover it, and we'll help you respond. Because the data belongs to your business, you are normally the one who must notify your customers and any government agencies; your contract explains who does what.

Our accessibility and security checks describe what we tested. They are not legal advice, and they do not certify that a site or app complies with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the New York State or New York City Human Rights Laws, privacy laws or any other law. We can't promise search rankings, sales or other business results. Content added after launch, such as new photos or PDFs, can affect accessibility, which is why our care plans include re-scans. No website or app can be made completely secure. We reduce risk, but we can't guarantee against breaches, outages or data loss.
